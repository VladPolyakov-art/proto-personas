# Interview notes (2 informal conversations, written up by the founder)

## Interview 1: line cook, 3 years at a 40-seat restaurant
- Swaps happen in the staff WhatsApp group. Says: "we just post it in the group and hope somebody answers."
- Manager sometimes finds out about a swap only when the person does not show up.
- Does not look at work apps during the day; checks the phone on the bus before the shift.
- Would not install a new app unless the manager makes everyone do it.

## Interview 2: shift manager, 2 years, same restaurant group
- Spends about an hour every week fixing the schedule after informal swaps. (Self-reported, not measured.)
- Says: "I don't care how they swap, I care that I know who is on the floor."
- The owner decides what software is bought; the manager can only recommend.
- Tried a scheduling app last year, staff stopped using it after a month.

Nothing about bartenders or servers, nothing about owners, nothing about devices or budgets.
