# Competitor notes (desk research, October 2026)

## When I Work
- Pricing page: per-user pricing for managers' teams; claims "scheduling in minutes".
- Feature list: shift swap requests, manager approval, push notifications.
- Review excerpt (app store, 2 stars): "Great for the boss, but I never know if my swap request was seen."

## Deputy
- Marketing page claims "reduces no-shows by 30%" (no study cited).
- Feature list: shift swaps, availability, time clock, payroll export.
- Review excerpt (forum thread, undated): "Too many features, I just want to swap my Friday."

## WhatsApp group (the substitute)
- Not a product category we researched; named in interviews by both interviewees as what they use today.
- Free, everyone already has it. No approval step, no record of who agreed to what.
