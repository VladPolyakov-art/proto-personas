# ShiftSwap: brief

A mobile app for restaurant teams to swap and cover shifts without a WhatsApp group.

## Who
- Restaurant staff (servers, line cooks, bartenders) who need to give away or pick up shifts.
- Shift managers who approve swaps and need the schedule to stay covered. The restaurant owner pays for the subscription; staff use it for free.

## Scenarios
1. A staff member cannot work Saturday and posts the shift for someone to take.
2. A colleague sees the open shift and offers to take it.
3. The manager approves or rejects the swap and the schedule updates.
4. A manager has an unexpected gap tonight and needs cover fast.

## What we believe (not verified)
- Most staff are on Android phones.
- Managers will not pay unless swaps are approved inside the app.
- Staff check their phone only before and after shifts.

## Out of scope for v1
Payroll, tips, time clocks.
